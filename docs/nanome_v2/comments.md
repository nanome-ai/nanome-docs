# Comments & Annotations

Since release 2.7, comments and annotations share one panel: the **Comments** tool in the [Tools Panel](/nanome_v2/toolspanel). A comment can belong to the whole workspace, to a scene or to an annotation, and it can point at specific atoms. Comments appear for everyone in the workspace as they're posted, in Nanome XR and in [Nanome Web](/nanome_web/comments).

## The panel

- **Filter**: **All**, **Workspace** (comments on the workspace itself) or **Scene** (comments on the current scene). A count shows how many comments match.
- **Modes** (Editors only): **Text** places a text note in 3D, **Sound** records a sound note, and **Delete** removes notes.
- **Comment on this workspace**: the box at the bottom posts a new comment.

A dot on the Comments tool's button shows that there are unread comments.

## Threads

Each comment starts a thread. Threads support:

- **Replies**
- **@mentions** of colleagues by name
- **Reactions**: thumbs up, thumbs down and an emoji picker
- **Edit** and **Delete**, for the comment's author only. An edited comment is marked **(edited)**.
- **Watch thread** and **Unwatch thread**, to follow a thread's changes

A comment can carry an **atom chip**, a link to specific atoms. Selecting the chip highlights those atoms and zooms to them.

Each person can post up to 20 comments a minute.

## Text and sound notes

A **text note** has **Text**, **Size** and **Color**. A **sound note** has a **Title**, a **Record** button and a playback speed. Recordings can be up to 5 minutes long, and the recorder shows the elapsed and maximum time.

Pointing at a text note in 3D shows a tooltip with its text.

## Hiding annotations

Each annotation row has an eye button to hide or show it, and the panel header has **Hide all annotations** and **Show all annotations**. Hidden annotations stay in the workspace and can be shown again.
