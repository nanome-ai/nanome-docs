# Wrist Menu

The Wrist Menu is a small menu attached to your virtual wrist.

<IconGrid label="Wrist Menu buttons" :cols="3">
<IconCard icon="menu-2" title="Clock and menu" href="#time">The big button. Shows or hides the Main Panel.</IconCard>
<IconCard icon="sparkles" title="MARA" href="#mara">Opens the MARA assistant.</IconCard>
<IconCard icon="microphone" title="Mute" href="#mute">Mutes or unmutes your microphone.</IconCard>
</IconGrid>

<vimg src="nanome-v2/wristmenu.jpg" />

## <AppIcon name="menu-2" /> Clock and menu {#time}

The large button shows the time. Selecting it shows or hides the Main Panel. The **Menu** button on the off-hand controller does the same.

## <AppIcon name="sparkles" /> MARA {#mara}

Opens the [MARA](/mara/overview) assistant. It needs Editor access to the workspace.

## <AppIcon name="microphone" /> Mute {#mute}

Mutes or unmutes your microphone. **X** on the off-hand controller (the left controller by default) does the same.

When the headset hasn't been given microphone permission, the button is disabled. The **Mic** control in the [Users tab](/nanome_v2/mainpanel#user-settings) explains why.
