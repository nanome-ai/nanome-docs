# Wrist Menu

<vimg src="nanome-v2/wristmenu.jpg" />

The Wrist Menu is a small menu attached to your virtual wrist.

- **Time**: the large button shows the time. Selecting it shows or hides the [Main Panel](/nanome_v2/mainpanel).
- **MARA**: opens the [MARA](/mara/overview) assistant. It needs Editor access to the workspace.
- **Mute**: mutes or unmutes your microphone. When the headset hasn't been given microphone permission, the button is disabled. The **Mic** control in the [Users tab](/nanome_v2/mainpanel#user-settings) explains why.

Two controller buttons do the same jobs:

- The **Menu** button on the off-hand controller shows or hides the Main Panel.
- **X** on the off-hand controller (the left controller by default) mutes and unmutes the microphone.
