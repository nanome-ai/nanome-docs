# Wrist Menu

The Wrist Menu sits at the wrist end of your off-hand controller. It shows only in a headset, and hides while scenes play back. The Vision Pro beta has no Wrist Menu.

## <AppIcon name="menu-2" /> Clock and menu {#time}

The large button shows the Nanome logo and the time. Selecting it shows or hides the Main Panel, which opens in front of you. In right-handed mode, the **Menu** button on the left controller does the same. The right controller has no **Menu** button, so in left-handed mode the Main Panel opens from here.

## <AppIcon name="sparkles" /> MARA {#mara}

Opens the [MARA](/mara/overview) assistant, already listening. It needs Editor access to the workspace, and it's grayed out when MARA voice isn't available. The off-hand controller's secondary button (**Y**, or **B** when left-handed) does the same.

## <AppIcon name="microphone" /> Mute {#mute}

Mutes or unmutes your microphone. The off-hand controller's primary button (**X**, or **A** when left-handed) does the same.

When the headset hasn't been given microphone permission, the button is disabled. The **Mic** control in the [Users tab](/nanome_v2/mainpanel#user-settings) explains why.
