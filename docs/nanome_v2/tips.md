# Helpful Tips for Working in Nanome XR

Three controls that keep everyone's view and the spotlight lined up.

<UiList label="Tips">
<UiItem name="Position Molecules">Turn it on only for a moment, then off again. While it's on, the spotlight system won't work properly.</UiItem>
<UiItem icon="target" name="Recenter Self" href="/nanome_v2/scenespanel#scene-settings">In the Scene settings of the Scenes Panel: resets your position as the scene viewing origin. Editors only.</UiItem>
<UiItem name="Meta button">On Meta Quest, holding the Meta button recenters your view. Each person can do this before a session, and again if the spotlight is off-center for them.</UiItem>
</UiList>
