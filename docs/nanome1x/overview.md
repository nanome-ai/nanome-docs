# Nanome Classic Overview

Nanome Classic (v1.24) is the first generation of Nanome, a real-time collaborative molecular modeling platform for molecular biology and chemistry. It uses off-the-shelf Mixed Reality headsets such as the Meta Quest 3 and Meta Quest Pro, enabling scientists around the world to collaborate and manipulate molecular structures. With primary uses in the pharmaceutical industry, specifically in Structure-Based Drug Design, Nanome is used by many drug hunters such as medicinal chemists, computational chemists, structural biologists, and protein engineers to collaboratively design molecules.

Nanome is used by many of the world's leading pharmaceutical companies.
