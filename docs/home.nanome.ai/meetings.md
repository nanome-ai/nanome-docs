# Create a meeting link

At home.nanome.ai you can schedule a meeting for later, or make a quick link for a session now.

<Steps>

1. Log in to your account at [home.nanome.ai](https://home.nanome.ai).

   <vimg src="nanome-page/account-login-page.png" alt="The home.nanome.ai login page" />

2. On the dashboard, choose **Meetings** on the left.

   <vimg src="home-nanome/dashboard-menu.webp" alt="Meetings in the dashboard's left menu" />

3. Choose **Create One** in the section you need: **Meetings** to schedule a meeting for later, or **Quick Links** to set up a meeting link.

   <ScreenMap src="home-nanome/meetings.webp" alt="The Meetings and Quick Links sections, each with Create One" label="Meetings page">
   <MapCard icon="calendar" title="Meetings" href="#scheduled-meetings" side="top" area="0.4,1.5,48.4,97.4">Schedule a meeting for later.</MapCard>
   <MapCard icon="link" title="Quick Links" href="#quick-link" side="top" area="51.2,1.5,48.4,97.4">Make a link for a session now.</MapCard>
   </ScreenMap>

</Steps>

<ShotRow src="home-nanome/dashboard-buttons.webp" alt="Authenticate Device and Schedule meeting at the top right of the dashboard">

## <AppIcon name="calendar" /> Scheduled meetings {#scheduled-meetings}

<ShotRow src="nanome-page/create-scheduled-meeting.png" alt="The form for a scheduled meeting" crop="32.5,2,36.4,93.5">

A scheduled meeting has a date and time. Add details such as the room name (and an optional password), the event title and a description.

<UiList>
<UiItem name="Schedule" area="50.9,13.4,12.2,5.2">The tab for a scheduled meeting.</UiItem>
<UiItem name="Room Name" area="34.3,24.8,32.8,7.3">The name of the room.</UiItem>
<UiItem name="Require password?" area="34.3,32.5,10.7,3.1">An optional password.</UiItem>
<UiItem name="Event Title" area="34.3,43.5,32.8,7.5">The title of the event.</UiItem>
<UiItem name="Description" area="34.3,51.4,32.8,12.7">A description of the event.</UiItem>
<UiItem name="Date & Time" area="34.3,64.7,32.8,13.5">The date, and a start and end time.</UiItem>
<UiItem name="Save and Continue" area="50.8,87,16.3,5.3">Saves the meeting.</UiItem>
</UiList>

</ShotRow>

## <AppIcon name="link" /> Quick Links {#quick-link}

<ShotRow src="nanome-page/create-quick-link.png" alt="The form for a quick link" crop="32.6,25,36.4,66.8">

A quick link is a link you send to others so they can join your Nanome session now, or for as long as the link is active.
